# Before you call it done

Run this over the thing itself (the screen, the page, the copy), not over the plan. Answer each by looking. Say which ones failed and what you changed; a check that always passes is not being run.

1. Can anything be removed: an element, a colour, a line, a label, a number? Try it before adding anything. (Restraint)
2. Does any headline, button or short paragraph end with one word on its own line? Are headline lines close to the same length?
3. Is text or an element beside a circle or a pill given a few extra points of space?
4. Is every inset the same on every side (the optical extra beside a round edge aside), and do neighbours (title and avatar, date and text) share one baseline?
5. Is secondary text one step back and primary text at full strength? Does every piece of text on colour, light or a photo still read without effort?
6. Is colour only where it has a job: the one primary action, the one part that carries new information? Is there any decoration without a job?
7. Does it look like its platform, and does it show nothing the platform cannot do?
8. Are the edge cases here: empty, loading, error, long names, what a person without permission sees, and whatever each button opens next?
9. Is the copy plain B1 English, in sentence case, with Title Case only on English actions, and no "user", "error", "failed", "invalid", "Read more" or "this field is required"? Do errors say what to do next, do limits say what is possible, and does each label sit outside its field?
10. Is every value (size, opacity, colour, spacing) from the system, not an ad-hoc number?
11. Is every destructive action last, in its own group, quiet in the list, red only on the confirm step, confirmed, with a footnote?
12. Does hover only darken the background or add a hairline, and does nothing pop, jump, flicker or load white before it animates?
13. Does the content fit its frame: no accidental scroll for a few items, nothing cropped, nothing hanging out?
14. Is there warmth from colour or light anywhere (a tint, a soft gradient, a lit surface), or is it white boxes on white? Does every fact the person needs survive the removing?
